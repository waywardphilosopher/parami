## The Ethics of Care

We have three things we're going to talk about.

### A: Virginia Held on the Ethics of Care

Watch the following video, and make notes. [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b0XcQgXn-yM](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b0XcQgXn-yM)

### B: The Ethics of Care as Moral Theory

Go back to the text, and try to answer the question: **what, according to Virginia Held, are the major features of the ethics of care**? We'll do this in groups.

### C: Can Care Transform Us?

Answer the following two questions, drawing on Held's text, the video, and your own thoughts.

1. What are the main ways in which ***care happens*** within your own societies and social contexts?
2. Do you think that a greater emphasis on care could transform these societies and social contexts? If so, how?
