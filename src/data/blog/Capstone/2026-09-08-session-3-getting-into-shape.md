This is a list mainly for me to remember things I want to mention. I've read through your submissions on Canvas, and I've given you feedback. A few themes have come up a few times. I'll talk through some of these:

1. Time pressures, stress and time management
2. Deciding on a topic
3. Fear of getting it wrong
4. Keeping your project focussed (you are not writing War&Peace, or building a complete template for Myanmar's future, or writing a 4 season TV series, or a 7 volume academic monograph: this may be the start of your life's work, but it is not the whole of it).
5. Starting from what you know — your expertise and interests. A capstone is not a whole new building, it is a final stone in the great edifice you have been building.
6. The IRB process
