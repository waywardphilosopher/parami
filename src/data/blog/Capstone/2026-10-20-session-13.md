## CITI Training

CITI stands for Collaborative Institutional Training Initiative. It provides cross-institution training in research skills, and is particularly focussed on research with human subjects. At Parami University, you have access to this, and you are expected to complete the relevant courses before you start the research. There's a document called **How to register and join CITI courses.pdf** on canvas, and we'll have a look through.

Once you've signed up, you'll see a number of courses.

### Courses compulsory for all

These are courses that you need to do, whatever your research.

- Humanities Responsible Conduct of Research OR Social and Behavioral Responsible Conduct of Research

### Courses that may be useful for research with human subjects

If you are dealing with personal information, inviting participants to take part in your research, or doing any research with human subjects, you may also choose to complete the following.

- Researchers - Information Privacy & Security (IPS)
- OHRP: Participant-Centered Informed Consent Training
- Social-Behavioral-Educational (SBE) Comprehensive*

The CITI training gives you a basic framework for your research, but there may be some other optional modules that you could benefit from taking.

## Presentations

The presentations are due on the 10th November. This is a **hard** deadline. You must submit by then. I will be sharing the rubric in a few days, but we can get started thinking now.

- You have **up to** 10 slides, but you do not have to use them.
- The presentation is a chance to **share your ideas** with the class as a whole. Here, you should focus on the following:
  - Simplicity — don't make things over complex
  - Clarity — make it clear what you are saying and why. Structure your content clearly
  - Communication — the whole purpose of a presentation is to communicate to actual real, live human beings. Make sure you bear this in mind! As yourself for each slide: *what is this communicating*?
  - Accessibility — don't cram your slides with text that is hard to read, or with overwhelming amounts of content. See the slides ike the hand-rail as you go up and down stairs. You and your audience use them as a guide,  but they are not the main point.
  - AI — don't use AI images or text. Please don't!
- You will be presenting in the class while showing your slides. You have around 8 minutes to present, with a few minutes for questions. 
- You are expected to attend ALL other students' presentations.

The presentation dates are, as mentioned last week, 15th, 17th and 22nd November.
