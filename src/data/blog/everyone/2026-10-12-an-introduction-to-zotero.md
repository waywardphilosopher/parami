## About Zotero

If you are writing academic essays or doing academic research, you need the right tools for the job. And one of the best tools  is Zotero. 

Zotero is a free tool that acts as your personal research assistant. It allows you to do the following:

1. Collect research materials (from academic papers to newspaper articles) and store them on your local device
2. Organise your research materials
3. Manage your metadata (all that extra information about papers and articles such as author, publication date, title, publication etc.)
4. Cite while you write, in whatever format you want (MLA, APA)
5. Produce reference lists at a single click
6. Collaborate with others

Zotero does all this for free. It is a project of the non-profit Digital Scholar ([https://digitalscholar.org](https://digitalscholar.org)), which is dedicated to developing research and publishing tools for digital scholars.

## Why Use Zotero

If you have ever been in any of the following situations, this is where you would use Zotero:

1. I want to find some academic papers for my essay!
2. I want to save copies of these papers, and find them again easily.
3. I have lots of papers, but the citations are all over the place. I need to tidy up the metadata.
4. My internet is terrible. I need to have all the academic papers that I need at my fingertips, without relying on the internet.
5. I want a tool that can help me keep track of my research materials.
6. I hate citing things. I need a clever robot to help me!
7. I need to produce a reference list. It’s so hard!

## How to Get Hold of Zotero

So if any of these are you, then you should download Zotero. My advice is to download the **desktop** version instead of the mobile version. 

First of all, go here: [https://www.zotero.org](https://www.zotero.org), and click the big button saying "Download."

Once you have installed Zotero, you should also install Zotero Connector. The download page [https://www.zotero.org/download/](https://www.zotero.org/download/) should detect your browser and give you the option to install the right connector for your browser. 

- Zotero is the main software that manages your research database
- Zotero Connector is a little browser extension that allows your web browser to talk to Zotero.

Try installing both. 

> **Pro tip:** To use Zotero Connector, you need to have Zotero open on your computer in the background.
