## Three Positions: realist, textual and discursive
In this second part of the lecture, Hall sets out three positions we can take in relation to race: 

1. Realist
2. Textual
3. Discursive

In your breakout groups, I want you to return to the text (https://ereader.perlego.com/1/book/2397448/373) and to do the following:

a. Discuss in groups to establish a clear definition of these three positions: the realist, the textual and the discursive.

b. What does the view of race as *discursive* suggest about the history of race science?

c. What is Hall arguing (and what is he *not*) arguing about the cultural functions of science? 

d. What is Hall saying when he argues that the body is a "text"

e. What do you think Hall means by conducting politics "without a guarantee"?
