## Welcome back!
Welcome to this class. We're going to start with an exercise working in pairs on dialogical notebooks. 

##  Dialogical Notebooks
What I'm going to do first of all is ask you to look again through the text you have read for homework: https://ereader.perlego.com/1/book/3480537/45 (chapter 40)

### Phase 1:
In the first ten minutes, I want you to re-read, and to select a passage of no more than 100 words that you want to share. When you have done that, cut and paste the section, and post it online on the new "dialogical notebooks" padlet. Make sure you use your real name on the padlet — as this will be important later. Find a suitable image as well if you like!

Once your passage is posted, write a response to this passage **using the padlet comment function.** 

### Phase 2: 
I'm now going to invite you to breakout groups, but **I don't want you to join the groups**. Instead, I'll share the window where it says who your dialogue partner is (or who your dialogue partners are). Take a note of who you are working with.

### Phase 3: 
You now have five minutes to write a response to your partner's padlet. Again, I'll share the timer.

### Phase 4:
In this phase, you're now going to respond to your partner, one again using the padlet. You have five minutes.

### Phase 5: 
Finally have five minutes to read through the dialogues.

 
## Feedback

We'll share these popcorn-style.

## Thinking through race and identity in Myanmar, Thailand and Beyond

Let's now discuss the text in more detail.  What I want you to do is to divide into breakout groups, and talk about the following questions:

1. Did anything in the text challenge your views of the history of race and ethnicity in Myanmar/Burma? If so, what?
2. How has bureaucracy impacted notions of race/ethnicity in Myanmar/Burma, both historically, and more recently?
3. What implications does the historical fact of extensive intermarriage in Burma/Myanmar have for how we think about race and ethnicity?
4. In what ways can Hall's notion of race as a "floating signifier" throw light on these historical processes?
