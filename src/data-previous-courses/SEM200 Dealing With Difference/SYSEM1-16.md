## Welcome back!

For your homework, I asked you to bring in some articles about race, ethnicity and identity in Myanmar / Burma, dating from the last five years. Today, we're going to have a small symposium, talking about these results.


## Presenting your article
First, we're going to present the articles that we have brought. You have ten minutes to make notes. Then you will each have a chance to share your screen, and talk through the article. You need to present on:

1. What the article is
2. Where it was published
3. Who the author is
4. Why you chose the article
5. What the main points made in the article are
6. Your responses to these main points

Each person will have up to 4 minutes to present on their article. There will be 1 minute for questions. 

## Thinking Through Race and Ethnicity
When we have all presented our articles, I'll put you into groups. In your groups, I want you to talk through your articles with your fellow group-members, and to come up with a list of problems or issues around race in Myanmar / Burma and the broader region. At the end, each group will share these problems as a list in the chat, and talk through them.

## Legislating for Equality and Justice in a Ethnically Complex Society
Your final task for today is to go back into your breakout groups. Imagine that you are putting in place **concrete policies** to address these problems. Now make a list of:

1. The policies you would put in place
2. Any objections you can anticipate towards these policies, and how you would respond to these objections.

We'll feed back at the end.
